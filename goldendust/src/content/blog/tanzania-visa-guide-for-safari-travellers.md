Almost every guest who comes on safari with us needs a visa for Tanzania. The good news is that it's simple, and you can do it all online before you fly. Here's how it works.

*Entry rules change from time to time. Always check the [official Tanzania immigration portal](https://visa.immigration.go.tz) or your nearest Tanzanian embassy before you travel.*

## Apply online, on the official site only

Tanzania's Immigration Department runs the official e-visa portal at [visa.immigration.go.tz](https://visa.immigration.go.tz). You fill in a form, upload a passport scan and a photo, and pay by card. When the visa is approved you get an email, and you print the grant notice or keep it on your phone.

Be careful with look-alike websites. Many sites offer to "process" your Tanzania visa and charge a lot more than the official fee. If the address isn't on immigration.go.tz, it isn't the government.

## What it costs

- **Most nationalities:** an ordinary single-entry tourist visa, US$50.
- **US citizens:** a multiple-entry visa, US$100.

Some nationalities don't need a visa at all, and a few need special approval before they travel. The portal shows the rules for your passport.

## How long it takes

Approval usually takes a few working days, but it can take longer at busy times. We suggest applying two to four weeks before you fly, once your flights are booked.

## Your passport

Your passport should be valid for at least six months after you arrive, with blank pages for the visa and entry stamps.

## Visa on arrival

Many nationalities can still buy a visa when they land at Kilimanjaro International Airport, the airport most of our safari guests fly into. It works, but after a long flight the queue can take a while. Applying online means you walk straight to the immigration desk.

## Yellow fever certificate

If you arrive from a country with a risk of yellow fever, or you spent more than 12 hours in transit at an airport in one, you'll need a yellow fever certificate. Read our [yellow fever guide](/post/yellow-fever-vaccination-tanzania-safari) for the details.
